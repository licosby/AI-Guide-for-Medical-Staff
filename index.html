Welcome

This project explores how healthcare professionals can evaluate AI-supported tools using the 5 pillars of HCAI. The goal is to help nurses, physical therapists, and medical students understand when an AI recommendation is safe, explainable, and appropriate for patient care.

What You’ll Find Here

  • Short, real-world case studies showing AI use in clinical settings
  • A click through "Is it safe?" guide to make sure it is a safe and ethical evaluation
  • An Explainability Viewer that breaks down AI recommendations
  • A Risk-to-Human Score Calculator to assess safety concerns

Why This Matters

AI is becoming more common in healthcare, but many clinicians are unsure how to judge whether an AI tool is trustworthy. This site provides simple, interactive tools to help medical staff make informed, safe decisions.
